
## construction finance 
This web-app is inspired by and based on the concepts taught in Columbia University's construction finance course. 
For the longest time i wanted to build this project, cause the developers ruin the fun the...moment they open a spreadsheet. (i still get nightmares about financial constraints being presented on a spreadsheet.)

Every bold architectural vision like the cantilevered balcony, the custom curtain wall, the generous public plaza, eventually gets dragged to the value-engineering butcher block. Not because the design is bad but because architects are rarely taught to defend their concepts in the language developers actually care about like internal rates of return, capital stacks and cap rates.

This platform flips that dynamic! Architects get to test financial feasibility directly into the early schematic design phase, they can stress-test their ideas, prove their value and protect the integrity of their projects before anyone else gets to chop them up.

Form follows function. And function follows finance. It's time we architects mastered all three.
